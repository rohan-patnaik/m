Husband’s staple swaps

  • Egg roti (16, 17, 24): beat 2 eggs, pour thin in a non-stick pan, about 1 minute a side.
  • Cauliflower rice (20): grate the cauliflower, dry-sauté 4–5 minutes.
  • Cabbage noodles (21–23): shred the cabbage thin, sauté 3 minutes.
  • Shirataki noodles in gravy (18 white sauce, 19 paneer mushroom gravy, 25 dahi chicken): rinse, dry-toss 2 minutes, simmer in the gravy 1–2 minutes.
  • Chicken, cooked twice: Sunday 18 for Sun + Mon, Sunday 25 (dahi chicken made with your curd) for Sun + Mon lunch.

Quantities are raw or dry weights. Husband (keto): 1,600–1,800 kcal, 150–165 g protein, 30 g net carbs or less, no roti or rice. Wife: one whey scoop; this week about 1,775–1,880 kcal and 100–130 g protein, no fruit, so nothing is wasted. Husband days over 30 g carbs get an orange mark.
